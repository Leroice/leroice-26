## My role

I led design at the program level. That covered direction, system contribution and keeping AU executives, the global product team and engineering connected. Early in the program I audited 34 flows and 360 screens. That audit is now the basis for the system decisions the rest of the work builds on.

## The design problem

Most multicurrency wallets look like either a banking app or a crypto app. Neither suits people who mostly use it to send money home. The wallet has to feel familiar enough to trust with money and light enough not to feel like a bank.
