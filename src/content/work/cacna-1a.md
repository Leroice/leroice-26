During a hospital visit I watched my son make art with no consequence. No audience, no goal. Just making.

My own practice had started to feel stale and I was looking for a new outlet, so I turned to 3D. It was a way to connect with my son and his disability on his terms.

I took his naive sketches and his way of working and turned them into splines. Then I explored texture and lighting in Redshift, which I hadn't used before. What came out were my own naive abstractions. We made art together and learnt about NFTs along the way.
