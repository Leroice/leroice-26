My role expanded to combine graphic design with end-to-end development of the ANZ Plus debit card experience. As the first physical touchpoint with ANZ's new digital bank, the card was designed to offer customers a warm welcome and introduce the brand.

We designed the card itself and the experience around it: ordering, tracking delivery, activating it and managing it in the app.

Applying for a card was 45% faster than ANZ's classic online application. Delivery went from three weeks to under four days. Over 60% of customers added their card to Apple Pay before the physical card arrived.

I also explored new looks for credit cards, using a clear substrate and overprinting. Both played on transparency: a card that's easy to pay back, and that shows you what's happening with your credit.

This included marketing concepts and a full system for how colour and substrate change with each card's features.
